# Image Slots

You can replace the current remote image URLs with your own uploaded files.

- Hero background: replace `assets/hero-crystal-bg.png`, or update `.hero-media` in `styles.css`.
- Personal crystal card: `assets/card-personal-crystal.png`.
- Emotional healing card: `assets/card-emotion-crystal.png`.
- Love crystal card: `assets/card-love-crystal.png`.
- Career and wealth card: `assets/card-career-crystal.png`.

Recommended sizes:

- Hero image: portrait or square, at least 1100px wide.
- Card images: landscape, at least 900px wide.

Put uploaded images in this `assets` folder and reference them like:

```html
src="assets/my-crystal-photo.jpg"
```

For the hero CSS:

```css
url("assets/my-hero-photo.jpg")
```
